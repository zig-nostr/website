# Privacy: the site, and what the apps talk to

This site collects nothing. There is no analytics script, no
tracking pixel, no cookie, and no third-party request. The only
thing a page writes to your browser is a `theme` entry in
`localStorage`, so the light/dark choice survives a reload.

The apps built on `zig-nostr` hold to the same standard: no
telemetry, no crash reporting, no phoning home beyond what is
listed here. Each network call they make on your behalf goes to a
destination you can see in the app or in the list below, and what
that destination learns is your IP address and the request you
made.

## Plaza

| Destination | When | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your relays, and the NIP-65 relays of people you follow | feed, search, publish | reading and writing Nostr |
| `search.nos.today`, `search.nostrarchives.com` | you type in the search field | NIP-50 people search |
| NIP-05 domains in profiles you open | profile view | verifying `name@domain` |
| The Blossom servers in your published list | you press Upload | picture uploads (EXIF and GPS stripped first) |
| `blossom.primal.net`, `blossom.band` | picture uploads, when you have no list of your own | built-in fallback media servers |
| `api.github.com/repos/zig-nostr/plaza` | every 6 h, switchable off in settings | update check |
| `njump.me` | only when you press share / open-on-the-web | renders a note as a web page to hand off |

Picture and link-preview fetches are URL-gated before any request:
no private, loopback or LAN addresses, no userinfo, no odd ports.
Optional proxying of images through a service you name (the
default suggestion is `wsrv.nl`) is off until you switch it on.

## Notary

The daemon talks to the relays in its own configuration, and to
nothing else. The approval API is loopback-only and answers only
with the token its parent app passed it on startup. An export of
your key requires the passphrase every time, is written to the
audit log, and the log file lives beside the key at mode 0600
(`SIGNER_LOG_FILE=""` turns it off).

## deed

deed talks only to what you asked it to: the relays and HTTP
endpoints named on the command line. `deed mcp` serves over stdio
only; publishing tools exist only when started with
`--allow-publish`, and the signing key is never a tool argument or
a result.

## What this site's builds load

Fonts and images are self-hosted. There are no CDN, font or
analytics origins in any page.
